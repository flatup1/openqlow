    ASHIKAN · 足関

    Japan's long leg lock tradition

    Japan did not invent leg locks. Catch wrestling and sambo grew their own. But Japan has been working on them for a very long time.

    1. 1882Kodokan judo is founded. Ashi-garami, the leg entanglement, is one of its joint techniques.
    2. LaterJudo takes it out of competition. Ashi-garami is still one of its four forbidden techniques.
    3. 1985 · 1993Shooto and Pancrase are born from shoot wrestling, where leg locks are a main weapon.
    4. 2000s →Fighters like Masakazu Imanari, nicknamed “Ashikan Judan” (10th dan of leg locks), carry them into MMA.

    FLATUP GYM is not affiliated with Masakazu Imanari or Imanari Jiu-Jitsu.

    FAQ

    Do I need experience?

    No. It's built for first-timers.

    Is there sparring?

    No. You drill each technique slowly with a partner who does not resist. You stop before any pressure. Nobody cranks a submission.

    Aren't heel hooks dangerous?

    At full speed, they can be. Here you drill the positions slowly with a cooperative partner and stop before any pressure. There is no sparring. Tap early, and you can stop at any time.

    Gi or no-gi?

    No-gi. This is jiu-jitsu for MMA, so there is no gi to grip.

    What language is the class in?

    Simple English, plus a translation app when we need it. Most of the class is taught by showing, so it's easy to follow.

    When can I come?

    We're flexible. Tell us your travel dates and a time that suits you, and we'll arrange the class around your trip.

    Price and payment?

    30 min ¥5,000, 60 min ¥9,000, 90 min ¥12,000 per person, for groups of 2 to 6. Coming alone: ¥7,500, ¥13,500 or ¥18,000. Payment is online, in advance.

    What if I need to cancel?

    Cancel up to 3 days before your class for a full refund. From 2 days before, there is no refund if you cancel for your own reasons. If we have to cancel, if the coach turns you away on the day for safety, or if your flight is delayed (show us the airline's notice), you get a full refund.

    Insurance?

    Please bring travel insurance that covers sports injuries. Many policies exclude martial arts, so check yours before you fly.

    What should I bring?

    A T-shirt or rashguard, shorts without zippers or pockets, a towel and water.

    Is this Imanari's gym?

    No. We are FLATUP GYM in Narita. We are not affiliated with Masakazu Imanari or Imanari Jiu-Jitsu.
