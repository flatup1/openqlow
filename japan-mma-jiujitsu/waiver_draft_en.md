## この下書きで気をつけたこと（日本語）

- **「ジムは一切責任を負いません」とは書いていません。** 日本の消費者契約法では、事業者の責任を全部なくす約束や、わざと・重い不注意のときの責任をなくす約束は無効になります。代わりに「危険があることを分かったうえで参加する」「ルールを守る」「法律で認められた権利は制限しない」と書いています。
- サイトの約束と同じ内容にしています: スパーなし／技は形をゆっくり練習し、圧をかける前に止める／タップのルール／飲酒・治っていないケガは不可／心臓・血圧は事前に医師へ／旅行保険のすすめ／ケガ・体調の情報はレッスン後に消す。
- **撮影は別に選べる**ようにしています（撮らない人も参加できる）。サイトの「記念動画は撮影に同意した人だけ」と合わせています。
- 健康の情報は「要配慮個人情報」にあたるので、使う目的と消す時期を書いています。**だれが・いつ消すかは、運用で決める必要があります。**
- 18歳以上が条件です（サイトと同じ）。
- 救急のときは119番に連絡し、病院に情報を伝えることへの同意を入れています。**ジムのAEDの有無と、近くの救急病院は未確認です。**

## Waiver and Participation Agreement (draft)

**JAPAN MMA JIU-JITSU — operated by FLATUP GYM, Narita, Japan**

Please read this before your class. Ask us if anything is unclear.

### 1. About the class
- This is a no-gi MMA jiu-jitsu class. There is **no sparring**.
- You will practice leg locks (including heel hook positions), the triangle choke and the armbar **slowly, with a partner who does not resist**.
- **We stop before any pressure.** Nobody finishes a submission on you.

### 2. Risks
I understand that martial arts training has risks, even when it is slow and controlled. These include sprains, strains, joint injuries (especially knees, ankles, elbows and shoulders), bruises, and in rare cases more serious injury.

### 3. My promises
- I am **18 years old or older**.
- I have **not drunk alcohol** today, and I do not have an injury that has not healed.
- I have told the coach about any injury, illness or health condition that could matter. If I have heart or blood pressure problems, I have asked a doctor before booking.
- I will **tap early** (tap my partner or the mat, or say "tap" or "stop") when I feel pressure or pain.
- I will **stop at once** when the coach or my partner taps or says "stop".
- I will follow the coach's instructions and will not use force or speed.
- I may stop or sit out at any time.

### 4. If someone gets hurt
- The coach may stop my class at any time for safety.
- In an emergency, I agree that the gym may **call an ambulance (119)** and give the hospital the information I wrote on this form.
- I understand that medical costs are mine to pay, and that the gym recommends **travel insurance that covers sports injuries**.

### 5. My health information
- The gym uses the health information I give only to plan a safe class and for emergencies.
- The gym **deletes it after the class** (except where the law requires the gym to keep it, or if an accident happened and the record is needed).

### 6. Photos and video (choose one or more)
- [ ] I am **happy to be filmed** for my own class video (sent only to me).
- [ ] The gym **may use** photos/videos of me on its website and social media.
- [ ] **Please do not film me.**

You can change your mind later by telling us. Joining the class does not depend on this choice.

### 7. Your rights
Nothing in this form takes away any rights you have under Japanese law. This form is governed by the laws of Japan.
