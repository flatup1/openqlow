# EP13「きのうの、じぶん。」— Image-to-Video（i2v）プロンプト集

**生成ツール**: Runway ML / Pika / D-ID / Haiper（予定）  
**入力形式**: 静止画 1024×1024 + i2v プロンプト  
**出力形式**: 動画 1080p / 30fps  
**尺**: カット別に指定

---

## C01｜ノアが華やかに、ミオがぎこちない（4秒）

**i2v プロンプト**
```
camera: wide shot of gym floor, capturing Noah and Mio side-by-side, 
motion: Noah executing smooth confident kick combination (华丽) in 0.5s, 
Mio attempting same kick but movement is stiff, awkward, taking longer (1.5s),
contrast: Noah's fluid grace vs Mio's struggling awkwardness,
facial expression Noah: confident cheerful, Mio: concentrated and struggling,
body language: Noah relaxed and natural, Mio tense and forced,
background: other children watching, clapping for Noah,
lighting: warm purple-tinted evening light stable,
overall tone: comparison visualization - the gap between them made visible,
animation intensity: medium (active motion focus, clear comparison)
```

### 動作指示
- ノアは流暢に技を決める
- ミオは同じ技でぎこちなく、止まったり戻ったりする動き
- 周りの拍手がノアに向く
- 対比が明確に見える

## C05★｜ミオが過去の自分を見る（★核ショット・ループ可・6秒）

**i2v プロンプト**
```
camera: medium close-up of Mio's face, static, focusing on expression transformation,
motion: minimal physical - this is ALL about expression and internal shift,
face animation:
  - 0-1.5s: eyes widen in surprise seeing past self, eyebrows raise slightly,
  - 1.5-3s: realization dawns (こんなにできなかったんだ), eyes brighten,
  - 3-6s: entire face softens into peaceful knowing smile, understanding settled,
eyes: from shocked wide to warm intelligent with recognition of growth,
subtle details: not judgment but self-compassion, not comparison but growth-seeing,
mouth: transitions from O-shape surprise to gentle knowing smile,
lighting transition:
  - start: soft cool gray (confusion, lack of self-awareness),
  - middle: gradual warm golden glow (realization of progress),
  - end: soft golden-pink enlightened glow (self-compassion, growth recognized),
breathing: stabilizes from anxious to calm, deep, centered,
body language: posture gradually straightens as understanding settles, shoulders relax,
supportive figures: Sandbag and Flat-chan visible in background, witnessing transformation,
overall tone: transformation moment, growth redefinition, self-discovery, 
NOT: self-criticism or judgment - instead: gentle growth awareness,
animation intensity: low (expression-focused, showing inner growth realization),
loop capability: FULL (entire 6s loops seamlessly, creating extended contemplation moment)
```

### 核ショット（本話最重要）
- 「昨日の自分を超えた感覚」の実体化
- ミオが「ちょっとでも前より進んでいた」ことを初めて気づく
- 比較対象の転換（隣の子 → 昨日の自分）が顔に表れる
